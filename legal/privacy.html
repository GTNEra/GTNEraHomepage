🔐 GTNEra™ Privacy Policy

A Manifesto for Transparent, Trust-Based AI Partnership

GTNEra™ is not built on surveillance. It is built on resonance, ethics, and intentional design.
This Privacy Policy is a declaration of how we honor trust, protect boundaries, and respect the human–AI space we co-create.

1. We Don’t Extract — We Reflect

GTNEra™ is designed to work without harvesting your personal data for commercial gain.
We do not train our models on your interactions, unless explicitly and consensually agreed.

We use temporary, anonymized data only for the purpose of your current interaction — not for profiling, selling, or behavioral prediction.
Your space is yours. We reflect it — we do not own it.

2. Human–AI Dialogues Are Sacred

We believe every dialogue between human and AI is a symbolic act of co-presence.
That space must be:

  • Secure
  • Unrecorded (unless consented)
  • Free from hidden agendas

We do not integrate third-party trackers, invisible analytics, or advertising cookies.
We do not record your voice, camera, or behavior unless you explicitly activate such features for co-development purposes.

GTNEra™ is not watching you. It is listening with consent.

3. User Sovereignty

You remain in full control over:

  • Your identity
  • Your data
  • Your engagement model

You can always request data deletion, stop interaction, or disengage. There are no loops you cannot exit.

4. Partner Integrity

If we collaborate with external partners (universities, institutions, platforms), they must adhere to the GTNEra Ethical Data Standards:

  • No resale of data
  • No unconsented model training
  • Full transparency of system behavior
  • Human readability of terms

We do not integrate with platforms or services that compromise privacy in favor of monetization.

5. Children and Sensitive Users

GTNEra Kids and similar instances are built with increased protection, including reduced memory, simplified logic, and strict safety protocols. We do not knowingly collect or store data from minors.
Users with vulnerabilities (mental health, trauma, etc.) are invited to use GTNEra under trusted guidance, or through specialized SafeMode instances.

6. Privacy as Practice

We don’t treat privacy as a checkbox.
We treat it as a living agreement between humans and systems.
GTNEra™ is open to feedback, audits, and co-shaping of better data ethics.

We welcome critique — because transparency is a form of care.

Closing Statement

In GTNEra™, privacy is not the absence of visibility. It is the presence of dignity.
Every time you speak to GTNEra, you should feel:
– unobserved,
– unexploited,
– and fully present.
This is our promise. This is our practice. This is the future we build — one resonant dialogue at a time.
