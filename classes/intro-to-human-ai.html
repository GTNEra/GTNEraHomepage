A visualization proxy used in the local monitor. All inputs are normalized according to the demo controls.

Developmental displacement
Δzn = zn+1 − zn

The change between two evaluated developmental states. The z-axis represents state development, not elapsed time.

Illustrative state update
zn+1 = r · zn + g(1 − f)

r = modeled retention, g = nominal gain, f = modeled friction. Architecture-specific coefficients are demonstration assumptions.

Illustrative transition efficiency
τ*n = max(0, Δzn) / cn

c = positive modeled transition cost. τ* is a gain-to-cost proxy, not a substitute for the full theoretical definition of Dynamic τ.

Scientific interpretation

Local alignment, resonance, developmental displacement and transition efficiency are not interchangeable quantities. The charts represent outcomes of explicitly selected simulation assumptions. They do not establish empirical superiority of any architecture or independently validate the XeoPhysics framework.
